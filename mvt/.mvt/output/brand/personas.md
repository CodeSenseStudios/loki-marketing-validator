# Personas

### Evidence-Seeking Founder
- Role: Founders and GTM leads
- Pain points: Pain point: Teams confuse enthusiasm with real demand
- Goals: Reduce guesswork; Validate demand before build
- Channels: LinkedIn, Founder communities

### Revenue Operations Lead
- Role: Ops lead in a growing SaaS team
- Pain points: Hard to prioritize experiments; Weak attribution clarity
- Goals: Show efficient acquisition paths; Reduce wasted spend
- Channels: LinkedIn, Email, Reddit
