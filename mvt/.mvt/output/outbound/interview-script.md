# Interview Script

## Intro
Thank them, explain the research context, and ask permission to take notes.

## Screening
Confirm role, team context, and whether they live with the target problem.

## Problem Exploration
Ask for the last time the problem happened, its frequency, and current workarounds.

## Current Solution
Ask what they use now, what it costs, and what frustrates them.

## Solution Reaction
Share a short concept, then ask if they would use and pay for it.

## Close
Ask to join the waitlist and refer another relevant person.
