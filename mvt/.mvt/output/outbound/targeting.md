# Targeting

- Company criteria: SaaS, 10-500 employees, recurring acquisition experiments
- Person criteria: Founder, Growth Lead, RevOps Lead, Product Lead
- Where to find them: Sales Navigator, community directories, attendee lists
- Volume targets: 50 emails, 50 LinkedIn DMs, 5 interviews
- Benchmarks: 5-10% email replies, 15-25% LinkedIn acceptance
