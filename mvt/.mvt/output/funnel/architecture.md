# Funnel Architecture

[Ad/Post] -> [Landing Page] -> [Email Signup] -> [Welcome Email] -> [Value Email D+2] -> [Social Proof Email D+5] -> [Urgency Email D+10] -> [Survey Email D+12] -> [Intent Signal Captured]

## Referral Loop
Each signup gets a shareable link. Successful referrals move them up the waitlist and unlock early access messaging.

## Experiment Hypothesis
We expect at least 3.0% of visitors to sign up at a CAC below $5.

## Metrics Per Stage
- Visits
- Landing page conversion
- Waitlist signups
- Email opens and clicks
- Survey completions
- Interview score

## Kill Criteria
- CAC > 3x target after meaningful click volume
- Conversion rate < 0.5% after 500+ visitors
- Budget > 80% spent with < 20% of target signups
